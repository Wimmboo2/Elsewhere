Elsewhere, for developers

Everything needed to rebuild the app in Jetpack Compose: tokens for both themes, the component set with its states, and every animation with its trigger, duration and easing. Values are in dp and sp. Open the interactive prototype next to this page; its motion log names the same tokens used below.

Color

Light is the Organic ground. Dark is a warm dusk, not an inversion: brown-black surfaces, the terracotta lifted one step so it reads at night, text on accent fills flips to ink, and the active state turns the ground deep moss. Shadows disappear in dark; a 1dp edge does their job.

Elevation

Two levels, on purpose. Level 0 is everything: flat tonal surfaces separated by color, never by shadow. Level 1 is the bottom sheet only: light theme one shadow (y −8dp, blur 28dp, ink at 16%), dark theme no shadow, surfaceContainer plus a 1dp top edge at 10% ink. The scrim is 42% (light) or 60% (dark) and is the only full-screen translucent layer.

Motion tokens

One easing set, four springs. Every transition lands between 120 and 420ms. Only transform, alpha and clip are animated, through graphicsLayer. The three ambient loops are the single exception to the duration cap: they are idle decoration, not transitions, and they switch off under reduced motion.

Rhythm and loops
Stagger 90ms lead, then 30ms per row. The index is capped at 7 and only the first 12 rows animate, so the last visible row starts by 300ms however long the list is.
Active ripple 3 rings, 1800ms each, offset 600ms, scale 1 → 1.85, alpha 0.45 → 0, emphasized decelerate.
Active spin the cookie shape turns once every 24s, linear. Pauses (not resets) on Stop, so the morph back to a circle hides it.
Skeleton pulse alpha 1 ↔ 0.45, 900ms, alternate, standard easing.
Reduced motion read ANIMATOR_DURATION_SCALE once and expose it as LocalReducedMotion. When on: every spatial move becomes a 150ms alpha crossfade, loops stop, the ripple is replaced by one static halo at 22% alpha, springs are skipped.
